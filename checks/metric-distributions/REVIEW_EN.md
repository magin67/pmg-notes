## Terminology and editorial review

The translation follows the English core specification and glossary: effective resistance, resistance metric, affine vector, grade, exterior object, decomposable object, metric evaluation, normalized potential, spanning-tree coefficient, and contraction.

“Norm” retains the PMG quadratic convention. An edge's own resistance is distinguished from the effective resistance between its endpoints in the whole network. “Cross-edge” is used for the square diagonal so that it is not confused with a graph-theoretic bridge. Auxiliary edge orientations are distinguished from reversal of input vectors.

Relative sensitivity refers to a relative conductance change, not to an elasticity normalized by the value of the form. Existing-edge distributions do not represent all possible additions. At higher grades, individual-edge influences are sums of set contributions; no inverse recovery from that field alone is asserted.
