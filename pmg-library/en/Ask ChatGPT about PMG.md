If you encounter unfamiliar notation in the Polyform Metric Geometry (PMG) library, want to understand a proof, or need to check whether a formula applies to your problem, ask ChatGPT. You can ask in English or Russian.

Copy the prompt below into a new chat and replace the line in brackets with your question. If you are reading a specific note, include its link. If your question is about a formula, paste the full formula and its notation.

```text
Answer my question using the published Polyform Metric Geometry (PMG) library:
https://magin67.github.io/pmg-notes/

Open and read the relevant notes. Use the library's notation and explain the symbols needed for the answer. When giving a formula, state its assumptions and check whether they hold for my problem as far as the information provided allows.

Give direct links to the sections that support your main claims. If you can link only to the note, give the section title.

Identify which results the library proves, which statements it illustrates with examples or checks computationally, and which it states as conjectures. If you derive a result yourself, show which results it follows from. If you cannot find support in the material you read, say so explicitly.

My question:
[Write your question and include a note link or formula if relevant.]
```

## Example questions

- In PMG, how does the effective resistance between two vertices differ from the length between them?
- When does the Greenian update formula cease to apply after deleting an edge? What happens to the denominator?
- Is there a proof that a single spectral polynomial distinguishes all open hexagonal SLR chains? What exactly does the computational check establish?
