## Thematic sections

- **[[en/Polyform Algebra and Combinatorics/Polyform Algebra and Combinatorics|Polyform Algebra and Combinatorics]]**. What objects does polyform algebra construct, which identities do they satisfy, and what do their coefficients count?
- **[[en/Resistance Geometry and Duality/Resistance Geometry and Duality|Resistance Geometry and Duality]]**. How are spaces, the resistance metric, geometric characteristics, and dual representations organized?
- **[[en/Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra/Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra|Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra]]**. Which features of a graph do its characteristics and decompositions retain? What can be reconstructed from them?
- **[[en/Graph Transformations and Gluing/Graph Transformations and Gluing|Graph Transformations and Gluing]]**. How do characteristics change when couplings vary, vertices are eliminated, or a graph is assembled from parts?
- **[[en/Directed Graphs and Extensions/Directed Graphs and Extensions|Directed Graphs and Extensions]]**. What changes when couplings become directed and constructions become nonsymmetric?

**“Spectra of Isomers”** is complete in its current bilingual scope: nine substantive notes and [[en/Graph Transformations and Gluing/Spectra of Isomers/Spectra of Isomers - Results and Open Questions|Spectra of Isomers: Results and Open Questions]]. Its primary home is [[en/Graph Transformations and Gluing/Graph Transformations and Gluing|Graph Transformations and Gluing]], alongside Chains of Cores, with a secondary entry through [[en/Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra/Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra|Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra]]. The Russian collection has passed a series-wide review. All notes, the navigator, and labels for 35 figures are available in English; full scalar distinguishability remains an open research problem.

## Finding connections and future directions

[[en/Cross-cutting Tags|Cross-cutting Tags]] provide ten specific topics for finding connections across series. A navigator normally needs two to four tags. Tags imply neither prerequisites nor completion of a research direction.

The [[ru/Карта библиотеки PMG|research map, in Russian]] retains direction codes, preparation status, and open questions. A direction may span several series, and a series may cover several directions. The thematic contents and research map are coordinated views serving different purposes.
