## Using tags

A series is placed by its main question; secondary entries identify substantial connections to other topics. Tags describe content: using a determinant alone does not make a series algebraic. General words such as “graphs”, “polyforms”, and “mathematics” are omitted because they scarcely distinguish materials in this library.

Russian and English names form the pairs in the table. Use these spellings without adding synonyms for the same topic. “Reconstruction” also includes negative results: the tag does not assert unique recoverability.

Reading order is specified by section pages and series navigators. Tagging a planned direction would not make it a published series; its status belongs in the research map.
