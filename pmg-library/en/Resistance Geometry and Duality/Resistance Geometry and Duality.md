## Reading route

The prerequisites are the Laplacian, Green matrix, resistances, and algebra of forms from [[en/Getting Started and Foundations/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|Introductory Series]].

1. [[en/Resistance Geometry and Duality/Spaces/Spaces|Spaces]] introduces spaces, coordinates, and operations on selected vertices.
2. [[en/Resistance Geometry and Duality/Metric parameters/Metric Parameters of Graphs|Metric Parameters of Graphs]] connects this geometry with the radius, centralities, and other characteristics.
3. [[en/Resistance Geometry and Duality/Greenian and polyform duality/Greenian and Polyform Duality|Greenian and Polyform Duality]] develops dual representations and metric potentials. Its algebraic route can also begin directly after the introductory series.

For the transition to measurements, read [[en/Resistance Geometry and Duality/Electrometry/Electrometry|Electrometry]]. The series starts with measured quantities and proceeds to forward sensitivity and inverse calculation; each note states the required assumptions and limitations.

For prescribed boundary values, read [[Selected Vertices and the Dirichlet Problem]], then proceed to boundary data. For changes to the metric, use [[en/Graph Transformations and Gluing/Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations/Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations|Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations]].
