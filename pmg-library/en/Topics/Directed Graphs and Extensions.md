## Reading route

The prerequisite is [[en/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|PMG Introductory Series]], especially the algebra of forms and the Laplacian exponential.

1. Start with balance and the bilinear Laplacian in [[en/Balanced graphs/Balanced Graphs|Balanced Graphs]], then proceed to the exponential and circulation.
2. For directional geometry, first use [[en/Spaces/Spaces and Subspaces in PMG|Spaces and Subspaces in PMG]], then read about resistances, coordinates, and the sphere in the balanced series.
3. For directed data, proceed to the oriented extensions in [[en/KVP decomposition/KVP Decomposition and Oriented Extensions|KVP Decomposition and Oriented Extensions]]; for forest toponomes, use [[en/Partition toponomes/Partition Toponomes|Partition Toponomes]] and [[en/Polynomial toponomes/Polynomial Toponomes|Polynomial Toponomes]].

Balance does not imply symmetry. Results from the symmetric spectral series and positive resistance geometry require separately checked conditions before being extended.
