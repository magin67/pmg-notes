## Reading route

The prerequisite is [[en/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|PMG Introductory Series]], especially operations on forms and the Laplacian exponential.

1. Begin with [[en/Structural identities/Structural Identities|Structural Identities]]: partition forms and their relations.
2. Continue with [[en/Symmetric polyforms/Symmetric Polyforms|Symmetric Polyforms]] and [[en/Combinatorial potentials/Combinatorial Potentials|Combinatorial Potentials]]: symmetric sums and counts of their representations. The combinatorial definitions can be read alongside the fundamental theorem.
3. Follow [[en/Partition toponomes/Partition Toponomes|Partition Toponomes]] with [[en/Polynomial toponomes/Polynomial Toponomes|Polynomial Toponomes]]: reduction to types, then polynomial evaluations.

For gluing applications, proceed from structural identities to [[en/Boundary polynomials and graph gluing/Boundary Polynomials and Graph Gluing|Boundary Polynomials and Graph Gluing]]; completing every other algebraic series is not a prerequisite for that route.
