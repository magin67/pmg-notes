## Reading route

The prerequisite is [[en/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|PMG Introductory Series]]. Inverse variations require [[en/Greenian and polyform duality/Greenian and Polyform Duality|Greenian and Polyform Duality]]; geometric operations use [[en/Spaces/Spaces and Subspaces in PMG|Spaces and Subspaces in PMG]].

- **Changing couplings:** [[en/Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations/Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations|Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations]], from one conductance to joint changes and constraints.
- **Assembly from parts:** [[en/Structural identities/Structural Identities|Structural Identities]] → [[en/Boundary polynomials and graph gluing/Boundary Polynomials and Graph Gluing|Boundary Polynomials and Graph Gluing]] → [[en/Chains of cores/Chains of Cores|Chains of Cores]]. Structural identities supply the language of partitions, boundary polynomials specify the retained data, and chains apply them to sequential assembly.
- **Preserved parameters:** before the matroid results on chains, read the definitions and gluing laws in [[en/Matroid parameters and forest characteristics of graphs/Matroid Parameters and Forest Characteristics of Graphs|Matroid Parameters and Forest Characteristics of Graphs]].

Characteristic polynomials additionally require [[en/Spectra and diagonalization in polyform algebra/Spectra and Diagonalization in Polyform Algebra|Spectra and Diagonalization in Polyform Algebra]] and [[From the Forest Passport to Spectral Boundary Data]]. The full forest passport, spanning-tree layer, and spectral passport address different problems.
