## Reading route

The prerequisite is [[en/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|PMG Introductory Series]]. Dual forest parameters also require definitions from [[en/Greenian and polyform duality/Greenian and Polyform Duality|Greenian and Polyform Duality]]. The section has three independent entry points:

- [[en/Matroid parameters and forest characteristics of graphs/Matroid Parameters and Forest Characteristics of Graphs|Matroid Parameters and Forest Characteristics of Graphs]] - which parameters depend only on the cycle matroid.
- [[en/KVP decomposition/KVP Decomposition and Oriented Extensions|KVP Decomposition and Oriented Extensions]] - how to decompose a chosen table of pair characteristics; the initial route requires linear algebra, without the whole matroid series.
- [[en/Spectra and diagonalization in polyform algebra/Spectra and Diagonalization in Polyform Algebra|Spectra and Diagonalization in Polyform Algebra]] - eigenvalues together with eigenspaces, operator reconstruction, and information lost under projection.

**Route towards spectra of isomers:** [[en/Matroid parameters and forest characteristics of graphs/Matroid Parameters and Forest Characteristics of Graphs|Matroid Parameters and Forest Characteristics of Graphs]] → [[en/Chains of cores/Chains of Cores|Chains of Cores]] → the planned series “Spectra of Isomers”. ## Future directions

“Spectra of Isomers” is the selected continuation, not yet a published series. Its main question is which configuration features spectral data can distinguish when matroid parameters agree. Transfer methods connect it to gluing; decoding and injectivity connect it to reconstruction. Spectra of bipartite graphs and mutual projections form a separate future direction.
