## Foundations of the library

- **[[en/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|PMG Introductory Series]]**: ten notes, from the Laplacian and electrical networks to polyform algebra and the exterior exponential.
- [[en/Foundations/Core specification|Core Specification of PMG]]: definitions, notation, fundamental identities, and applicability conditions.

- [[Further Reading for the PMG Introductory Series]]: external sources for deeper study.

## Choosing a route

1. Read the introductory series in its navigator's order. For a particular problem, use the shorter routes given there.
2. Choose a question in the [[en/Topics/Topics|thematic index]]. Each section identifies supporting series and suggested transitions; detailed prerequisites appear in the series navigators.
3. For a computational introduction, open [[en/PythonExamples/PythonExamples|Programming Examples]]. The examples connect notation in the notes to code and expected results.

After the introduction, the geometric route leads to [[en/Topics/Resistance Geometry and Duality|Resistance Geometry and Duality]], the algebraic route to [[en/Topics/Polyform Algebra and Combinatorics|Polyform Algebra and Combinatorics]], and coupling changes to [[en/Topics/Graph Transformations and Gluing|Graph Transformations and Gluing]]. For comparing characteristics, choose [[en/Topics/Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra|Invariants, Decompositions, and Spectra]]; for directed couplings, choose [[en/Topics/Directed Graphs and Extensions|Directed Graphs and Extensions]].
