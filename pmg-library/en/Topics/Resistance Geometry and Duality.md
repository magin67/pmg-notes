## Reading route

The prerequisites are the Laplacian, Green matrix, resistances, and algebra of forms from [[en/Introduction/PMG Introductory Series|PMG Introductory Series]].

1. [[en/Spaces/Spaces and Subspaces in PMG|Spaces and Subspaces in PMG]] introduces spaces, coordinates, and operations on selected vertices.
2. [[en/Metric parameters/Metric Parameters of Graphs|Metric Parameters of Graphs]] connects this geometry with the radius, centralities, and other characteristics.
3. [[en/Greenian and polyform duality/Greenian and Polyform Duality|Greenian and Polyform Duality]] develops dual representations and metric potentials. Its algebraic route can also begin directly after the introductory series.

For prescribed boundary values, read [[Selected Vertices and the Dirichlet Problem]], then proceed to boundary data. For changes to the metric, use [[en/Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations/Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations|Laplacian, Greenian and Resistance Variations]].
